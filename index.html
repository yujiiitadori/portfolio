A Chrome extension for people with partial sight.

I had set out to build smart glasses, until a visit to a school for the blind showed me the real barrier wasn't the world outside — it was the screen in front of them.

Scrub through the 4 months

The pivot

Scroll sideways to follow the path.

i.

The glasses

Smart glasses and a voice app. Ambitious. But similar products already struggled on cost and adoption, and the hardware was out of reach for a solo project.

ii.

The site visit

Most tools are built for total blindness — expensive or unavailable in India. Many students sit in the gray areas. Streets weren't the problem; screens were.

iii.

The extension

Something I could build alone, that works on any website. Research stretched to 7 weeks to learn extensions from scratch.
